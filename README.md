# Fangs A Bot Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 5th, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Fangs A Bot** ("Fangs," "the bot," "we," or "us") handles information when you use the bot in a Discord server or send it a direct message. By using Fangs, you acknowledge the practices described below.

## Information We Handle

### Messages and commands

Fangs may receive and process messages, commands, and related Discord metadata when needed to respond, enforce configured bot behavior, or operate its features. Fangs does **not** store the content of server chat messages for message-history, analytics, or profiling purposes.

### Last-message timestamps

Fangs stores a timestamp associated with a user's most recent message. This timestamp is used only to support the bot's functionality, such as cooldowns, activity checks, or other configured automation. The timestamp is not the message content and is not used to build a user profile.

### Direct messages and ticketing

When you DM Fangs to create or manage a support ticket, Fangs may process and store the information needed to create, update, route, and track that ticket. This may include your Discord user ID, ticket status, and timestamps. Ticket information is used only to provide and administer the ticketing system.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- provide, maintain, and troubleshoot Fangs;
- respond to commands and direct messages;
- create and manage support tickets;
- prevent abuse and protect the bot and its users; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information or use stored information for targeted advertising.

## Data Sharing

Fangs does not share information with third parties except when necessary to operate the bot, comply with the law, protect rights and safety, or respond to a valid legal request. Discord independently processes data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

## Retention and Deletion

We retain last-message timestamps only for as long as reasonably needed for the bot's operation. Ticket records are retained for as long as needed to resolve, audit, and administer the ticketing system, after which they are deleted or anonymized according to our operational needs.

You may request deletion of data associated with your Discord account by contacting **U_Mage**. We may need to verify your request and may retain limited information where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, dispute resolution, or recordkeeping.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information handled by Fangs. No internet service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

Fangs is not directed to children under the minimum age required to use Discord in their country or region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Fangs or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted here with a revised “Last updated” date.
